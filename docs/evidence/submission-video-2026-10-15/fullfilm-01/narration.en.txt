Meet Mochi. Emma knows the little habits: a favorite feather toy, and a quiet rest after meals. Today, Alex is taking over. The question is simple: what does Alex need to know?

Emma adds that preference to PawTwin. It begins as a candidate memory. She checks it and confirms it, so the detail can stay with Mochi's shared record.

Alex opens a fresh conversation in the Alexa web simulator and asks what Mochi likes. The reply uses the confirmed preference, with its source available to inspect. A new conversation, with a familiar starting point.

Next, Emma asks for a playtime plan. She gets a draft to review, chooses the time, and prepares a proposal. At this point, it is still a suggestion. Only her explicit confirmation creates a shared manual care task, waiting for someone to carry it out.

Alex claims the task through his own account, then records it as completed. Each family member is working from the same agreed plan. His name and the saved result stay visible in the household history, so Emma can see who recorded the completion.

When Emma generates the daily report, it includes that care record. This is a summary of saved household records; it does not independently verify that physical care happened.
