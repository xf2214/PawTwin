Who is looking after Mochi today?
When everyone loves the same pet, care can still get lost between family members. Paw Twin gives Emma and Alex a shared daily routine for their fictional cat, Mochi.

Start with a shared view of the day
Emma opens the household application to see Mochi's records and today's care. Alex has a separate account, so family actions can be attributed to the person who records them.

Keep measurements tied to their source
Two sample weights, seven days apart, produce a report showing a hundred fifty gram increase. The calculation links to its records, while actual food intake stays unknown until someone records it.

Let the owner confirm what is remembered
Emma records that Mochi likes a feather toy and rests after meals. She confirms this preference before it becomes part of the shared context for future answers.

Alex can pick up the same context
Alex opens a new conversation in the Alexa web simulator and asks what Mochi likes. The reply reads the confirmed preference from the shared record and exposes the source for inspection.

Turn a request into a care draft
Emma asks the simulator to plan playtime and reviews the proposed care. She chooses a time, keeping the request as a draft while she checks the details.

Review before adding a task
The prepared proposal shows what will be arranged for Mochi. At this stage, no care task has been created; Emma still controls the final confirmation.

Confirm the shared care arrangement
Emma confirms the arrangement, creating one shared manual care task. The result says it is waiting for a person, keeping a planned action distinct from a completed one.

Alex takes over and records completion
Alex claims the task and records the playtime as completed through his own account. The household history identifies who recorded the result, helping the next family member understand the handoff.

See the result in the daily report
Emma's daily report now counts one completed manual care task. The report summarizes recorded care without inventing food intake or making a clinical diagnosis.

Share limited access with a caregiver
When someone else helps, Emma can issue time-limited, read-only access for one selected pet. The caregiver can inspect the shared context, while the owner keeps control over changes and revocation.

Bring the recorded story to a vet visit
Before a vet visit, Emma creates a dated information card from Mochi's records. It separates known facts from missing information and can be printed or saved as an offline document.

A shared routine the family can follow
Paw Twin connects records, confirmed care, family handoffs, and reports in one inspectable routine. This demonstration runs locally with rules and SQLite; Alexa and devices are simulated, and live cloud integration remains unverified.
