PawTwin — Care, with context
Full-film editorial draft, 15 October 2026. Target: approximately 152 seconds.
All people, pets and records in this demonstration are fictional.

00:00–00:08 — A household handoff
Alex is taking over Mochi's care today. What should he know?
The useful details should not get lost between family members.

00:08–00:25 — The owner decides what stays
Emma records a simple preference: Mochi loves the feather toy and rests after meals.
It starts as a candidate. Emma reviews it and confirms it.
Only then does it become part of their shared pet context.

00:25–00:45 — A fresh conversation, the same context
Alex opens a fresh conversation in the Alexa web simulator.
He asks what Mochi likes. The local app uses that same confirmed record.
The source remains available to inspect. Alex can pick up the context without asking Emma to repeat it.

00:45–01:18 — From a suggestion to an agreed plan
Emma asks for a playtime plan.
The first response is a draft. No care task exists yet.
She reviews the details, prepares a proposal, and explicitly confirms it.
The confirmed plan becomes a manual care task. It still needs a person to carry it out.

01:18–01:48 — The other member takes over
Alex claims the task and records its completion.
Emma sees the updated household record, and the daily report includes the completed care.
This is a recorded action by a family member; it does not independently verify physical care.
The handoff stays visible, from the agreed plan to the saved result.

01:48–02:24 — Pet Concierge, a scripted product preview
The same idea also shapes our Pet Concierge preview: ask a short question with this pet's context alongside it.
Miso's scripted example combines a food question with his profile and a confirmed preference.
Luna's example presents illustrative activity records and observation guidance.
These conversations and traces are presets. They are not live model responses, actual tracking or a diagnosis.
The comparison shows the goal: less repeated background, with the relevant context kept visible.

02:24–02:32 — Close
PawTwin. Less repeating. More caring.
Local records, with an Alexa web simulation.
