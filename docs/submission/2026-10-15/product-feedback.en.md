This draft does **not** report first-hand cloud onboarding, real latency, inference quality, reliability or actual live use. Cognito/S3 and cloud family deployment are not demonstrated. If the participant has separately performed a real authorized integration, add its dated evidence and first-hand feedback before claiming actual use. Otherwise keep these outside the live-service feedback list and do not use them to claim an AWS Builder bonus.
